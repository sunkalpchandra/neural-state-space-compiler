### R-03 [high] Latent-model early stopping / best-state restore uses a non-stationary validation loss (curriculum horizon), unlike the baseline trainer

- file: `src/nssc/training/trainer.py`
- problem: Trainer.evaluate() reuses self.loss_fn whose rollout_horizon was set in train_epoch() to the current curriculum horizon, so the val 'total' monitored for early stopping and best-checkpoint restore changes definition every epoch (horizon 1 -> rollout_horizon over epochs/2). Rollout MSE grows with horizon, so val/total rises during the curriculum for models whose rollout error grows with H, early stopping triggers, and the epoch-0/1 weights (trained at horizon 1-3) are restored and then evaluated at H=250-480. BaselineTrainer.evaluate() deliberately evaluates at the full horizon 'for a stable criterion', so the two model families are selected under different rules. This asymmetry is not in research/decisions.md or README, and it confounds the reported linear/PCA latent results and the compiler's screen-stage pruning.
- evidence: trainer.py:114 sets loss_fn.rollout_horizon = self._horizon_at(epoch); trainer.py:139-151 evaluate() uses the same loss_fn; trainer.py:168-177 monitor = va['total'], best_state restored, patience break. baselines/trainer.py:158-162 uses h = cfg.rollout_horizon (full) for validation. Actual histories: results/raw/benchmarks/synthetic_core/lorenz63/pca_linear_d3/seed0/history.json best epoch 0 (horizon 1, val 0.0075 -> 0.0169 -> 0.025 as horizon 1->2->3), stopped at epoch 16; linae_linear_d3 best epoch 4 (horizon 5), stopped at 20; results/compile/lorenz63/runs/screen: 38 of 85 runs restore best-state from the first quarter of 12 epochs (e.g. mlp+linear@d2 epoch 0, horizon 3). README:128-129 reports these as PCA+linear 1.010 / linear AE 'diverged', README:143-145 draws the 'linear dynamics diverge' conclusion.
- fix: Evaluate the validation loss at a fixed horizon (cfg.rollout_horizon, as BaselineTrainer does) or monitor a horizon-independent metric; record it in decisions.md and rerun the affected latent runs (synthetic_core linear/PCA models and the compiler screen stage), or explicitly declare the current behaviour and its effect on the linear-dynamics rows.
